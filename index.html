The philosophy

Juttr is not a blocker.

Blockers treat your curiosity like a problem. We treat it like inventory. Your ideas are not distractions — they just need the right time.

Not this

Block the internet and hope the impulse dies.

This

Capture the impulse in two seconds and return when you choose.

Not this

Another app to check, another inbox to forget.

This

The tab you already open 100× a day becomes the workspace.

Not this

Your saves and notes mined on someone's server.

This

Your workspace stays on your device — in a local folder you control.

Stays on your device

Tasks, notes, boards, calendar entries, focus records, settings, and the files you open. Stored in your browser and in a local folder you pick. Juttr does not receive this content.

All we hold about you

An email address for your account sign-in (Supabase), and a subscription record if you buy Pro (Stripe). That's it — no mailing list, no name, no tracking. The site runs on Vercel.

Free features work without an account. Read the detail in our Privacy Policy.
